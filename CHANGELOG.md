# Changelog

## 1.0.3
- The Saber now swings while you carry an objective that only allows one-handed weapons (such as the black box). Fire swings it with its normal swing animation instead of just holding it out, and the carry pose comes back after the last swing.

## 1.0.2
- Fixes a crash on the loadout screen ("attempt to index a nil value" in draw_perks) when opening a slot with the mouse on a profile with few unlocks, e.g. Grenades or Equipment on a fresh save. An open list now always keeps its selection inside the list.
- The log shows the right version again.

## 1.0.1
- Adds a mod icon in Obsidian. Game files unchanged.

## 1.0.0
- First release on GitHub, with update support in Obsidian.
